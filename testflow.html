7. Design Gaps Documented (3)

#GapSeverityDetailsRecommendation
1Write actions gated by REA, not CRE/UPD Medium POST /api/leads checks Sales REA instead of Sales CRE.
PUT /api/receipts (PDC status change) checks Finance REA instead of Finance UPD.
POST /api/bookings checks Sales REA instead of Sales CRE.
Viewers with REA can technically write. 		Use withPerm('module','CRE') for POST, withPerm('module','UPD') for PUT
2Stateless JWT — no server-side revocation Low Logout clears the cookie client-side only. Pre-logout JWT remains technically valid until expiry.
Same for portal logout. 		Add a token denylist (Redis/set) or short-lived rotating refresh tokens
3Duplicate broker reservations allowed Low POST /api/portal/broker reserve allows reserving an already-reserved unit (creates 2nd row). Add unique constraint on (unit_id, status='active') or check before insert
