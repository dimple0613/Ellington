# Developer inventory — Team & Org

> CEO operating plan for the Developer Inventory project.
> Reference design: `Archive/UI systems design review/Ellington ERP.dc.html`.

## Mission
One console to run the developer's sales-to-collection-to-handover operation:
inventory, pricing & availability, sales/leads/booking, finance/payments, collections,
escrow, cashflow, handover/snagging/deeds, plus an executive mobile app.

## Leadership
| Role | Lead | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | owner | vision, priorities, decisions |
| Head of Product | hire-1 | roadmap, module specs, workflows |
| Engineering Lead | hire-2 | architecture, delivery, codebase health |
